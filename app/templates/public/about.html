OUR STORY

A better place to work, one cafe at a time.

WorkCafe Pune is a growing guide to cafes around Pune that people can explore for work, study, and coffee.

Why WorkCafe?

Finding a cafe that suits a laptop session takes more than finding good coffee. Location, pricing, seating, Wi-Fi, and power availability can all influence where you decide to spend your time.

What you'll find here

We organise cafe listings by neighbourhood and provide useful details such as addresses, descriptions, price levels, and amenity information when available.

Honest information matters

We aim to make each listing useful and transparent. Information that hasn't been confirmed is marked as unverified instead of being presented as fact. Prices, amenities, and cafe policies may change, so visitors should confirm important details before visiting.
